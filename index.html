One training cycle, in plain English

1. Predict
Inputs travel through weighted connections. Each neuron combines its inputs, adds a bias and applies an activation function.

2. Measure the error
A loss function compares predictions with known answers. Lower loss means predictions fit the training examples better.

3. Adjust and repeat
Backpropagation finds how each weight contributed to error. Gradient descent updates the weights. One epoch uses all training examples.

Validation examples never update the weights. They help you judge whether the network learns a pattern that also works on unseen examples.
